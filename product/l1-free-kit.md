## 1. The 60-Second Enquiry Reply
*Never let a lead go cold because you were on a job.*

```
You are the owner of a [YOUR TRADE, e.g. plumbing] business in [YOUR SUBURB/CITY].
A potential customer just sent this enquiry:

"[PASTE THEIR MESSAGE]"

Write a warm, professional reply (4–5 sentences) that:
- Thanks them and shows I understood their problem
- Gives a realistic next step (e.g. a quick call or a time to visit)
- Asks the ONE most useful question to move toward a booking
- Sounds like a friendly local tradie, not a corporate robot
Keep it short enough to send as a text message.
```

---

## 2. The Instant Quote Draft
*Turn job notes into a clean quote in seconds.*

```
Write a professional quote for a [YOUR TRADE] job.

Job details: [DESCRIBE THE JOB — what, where, materials, hours]
My hourly rate: [$X]. Call-out fee: [$X]. Materials estimate: [$X].
Customer name: [NAME].

Format it as:
- A friendly one-line intro
- A clear itemised breakdown with a total (AUD)
- What's included / not included
- How long the quote is valid and how to accept
Keep the tone confident and easy to read. Australian spelling.
```

---

## 3. The Quote Follow-Up
*Most jobs are won on the follow-up nobody sends.*

```
I sent a customer a quote [X days] ago for [BRIEF JOB DESCRIPTION] and haven't heard back.
Write a short, friendly follow-up message that:
- Doesn't sound pushy or desperate
- Reminds them of the value, not just the price
- Makes it easy to say yes or ask a question
- Ends with a simple call to action
Two versions please: one for text, one slightly longer for email.
```

---

## 4. The 5-Star Review Request
*Every happy customer is a review you're leaving on the table.*

```
Write a warm message asking a happy customer to leave us a Google review.
My business: [NAME], a [YOUR TRADE] business in [SUBURB].
The job I just finished for them: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION].
- Keep it genuine and personal, not template-y
- Make leaving a review feel quick and easy
- Include a friendly line they could even copy as their review
Give me a text-message version and an email version.
```

---

## 5. The "We're Booked Out" Holding Reply
*Stay in the running even when you can't start right away.*

```
A customer wants [JOB], but my earliest availability is [DATE].
Write a reply that keeps them keen instead of sending them to a competitor:
- Be upfront and confident about the wait (in-demand = trustworthy)
- Offer to lock them in now / join a priority list
- Give a smaller helpful tip or interim option if relevant
Keep it friendly and under 6 sentences.
```

---

## 6. The Price-Objection Response
*Handle "that's too expensive" without dropping your rate.*

```
A customer said my quote for [JOB] is too expensive.
Write a calm, confident reply that:
- Doesn't get defensive or immediately discount
- Explains the value and what's included (quality, warranty, reliability)
- Offers a fair option (e.g. staged work, cheaper alternative) IF appropriate
- Leaves the door open without begging
Australian tone, friendly but firm.
```

---

## 7. The Google Business Profile Booster
*Show up higher when locals search for your trade.*

```
Write a Google Business Profile "update" post for my [YOUR TRADE] business in [SUBURB].
Recent job / season / offer: [WHAT'S HAPPENING NOW].
- 3–4 sentences, upbeat and local
- Naturally include the service + suburb so it helps local search
- End with a clear call to action (call / book / message)
Give me 3 different versions I can rotate.
```
