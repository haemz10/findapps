# Module 4 — Your 15-Minute Weekly SOP

Automations still need a captain. This is the entire ongoing routine — about 15 minutes,
once a week. Do it and the whole system keeps paying you.

---

## Every Monday (15 minutes)

**1. Open your `Leads` sheet (5 min)**
- Any leads still `New` with no reply? → reply now (Prompt #1).
- Any `Quoted` older than 3 days with no follow-up tag? → check the Quote Rescue ran.
- Any jobs finished last week marked `Completed`? → confirm a review request went out.

**2. Check the numbers (3 min)** — jot these in a row at the bottom of the sheet:
| Metric | This week |
|---|---|
| New leads | |
| Quotes sent | |
| Jobs won | |
| Reviews received | |

**3. Confirm the robots are alive (2 min)**
- Open Make.com → check each scenario shows recent successful runs (green).
- Send yourself a test enquiry once a month to confirm the auto-reply still fires.

**4. Send this week's review asks (5 min)**
- Any happy customer who slipped through automation → send Prompt #21 by hand.

Done. Close the laptop.

---

## Every month (20 minutes)

- **Re-engage old customers:** send Prompt #18/#19 to past customers for a seasonal service.
- **Reply to all reviews:** Prompts #23/#24 — Google rewards businesses that respond.
- **Post to Google Business Profile:** Prompt #26 (3 posts) — keeps you visible in local search.
- **Review your Leads sheet trend:** are leads going up? Which source is best? Do more of that.

---

## The numbers that matter (and what they tell you)

| If you see… | It means… | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Lots of leads, few quotes | Slow to reply / not qualifying | Tighten the auto-reply; use Prompt #6 |
| Lots of quotes, few wins | Price or follow-up gap | Check Quote Rescue is running; try Good/Better/Best (#9) |
| Few leads at all | Not visible enough | More reviews (#21), GBP posts (#26), ask for referrals (#25) |
| Wins but no reviews | Review engine off | Fix Sequence 1; ask manually this week |

## The 90-day goal

If you run this system consistently for 90 days you should see:
- **Reply time:** under 60 seconds on every enquiry
- **Reviews:** a steady trickle every week (aim: +8–12/month)
- **Recovered jobs:** at least 1–2 "dead" quotes won back each month
- **Your time back:** evenings free — the inbox works for you now
