# L3 — Simple Service Agreement (Template)

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** This is a plain-English starting template. Laws differ by state and
> country. Before using it in Australia, have it reviewed once by a lawyer or use a service like
> LawPath/Lawyer.com.au, and check your obligations under Australian Consumer Law (you can't
> contract out of consumer guarantees). Fill every `[BRACKET]`. Keep it short — clients trust
> clarity over 10 pages of fine print.

---

## Lead Response System — Setup Service Agreement

**Between:** [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] ("we", "us") — ABN [YOUR ABN]
**And:** [CLIENT BUSINESS NAME] ("you", "the client")
**Date:** [DATE]

### 1. What we'll do
We'll set up a lead-response system in your business within **72 hours** of receiving your
payment, completed onboarding form, and required access. It includes:
- Missed-call-to-text auto-reply setup
- A lead-capture Google Sheet connected to up to **two** enquiry sources
- An automated review-request sequence
- An automated 3-step quote follow-up sequence
- A personalised pack of message/prompt templates
- A 20-minute handover call and a 1-page cheat sheet

### 2. What's not included
Website builds, ongoing management, advertising, CRM migration, and connecting more than two
lead sources are **not** included and can be quoted separately. Ongoing monitoring is available
under our separate "Care" plan.

### 3. Price & payment
- Fixed fee: **A$490** (incl. GST if applicable — [confirm your GST status]).
- Payable [in full up front / 50% deposit to book, balance on handover].
- Payment method: [Stripe/PayID/bank transfer — link].

### 4. Your responsibilities
You'll provide accurate business details and the access we need (Google account, review link,
enquiry-source access) in a timely way. Delays in providing these pause the 72-hour timeline.
You're responsible for any third-party tool costs (e.g. SMS credits) billed under your own accounts.

### 5. Access & privacy
We'll only use the access needed to deliver the service. We won't store your passwords, and
we'll remove our access on completion unless you're on an ongoing Care plan. We'll handle your
information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

### 6. Guarantee
If, within **7 days** of handover, the system isn't working as described, tell us and we'll fix
it or refund your fee in full.

### 7. Things outside our control
The automations rely on third-party tools (e.g. Google, Make.com, your phone carrier, SMS
providers). We're not responsible for outages, price changes, or policy changes by those
providers, but we'll help you adapt if they change.

### 8. Liability
Our total liability is limited to the fee you paid. Nothing in this agreement excludes rights
you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

### 9. Ownership
Once paid, the setup and templates in your accounts are yours to use in your business. Our
underlying template library and methods remain ours.
