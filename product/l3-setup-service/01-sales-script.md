# L3 — Sales Scripts & Objection Handling

You don't need to be a salesperson. Local service owners buy this when they *feel the pain*
and *trust it's easy*. Your job is just to make those two things obvious.

---

## Where these clients come from
1. **Your L2 buyers** — anyone who bought the $59 kit but hasn't set it up in 2 weeks. Email them.
2. **Local Facebook groups / community pages** — where tradies and owners hang out.
3. **Direct outreach** — businesses with few or old Google reviews (they're clearly not asking).
4. **Referrals** — every happy setup client knows 5 other owners. Ask (Prompt #25).

---

## The DM / email opener (cold-ish)
```
Hi [NAME], I help [TRADE] businesses around [AREA] stop losing leads to missed calls.
I set up a system that auto-replies to every enquiry in under a minute, sends your quotes
faster, and gets you more Google reviews — installed in 72 hours, one flat fee, nothing for
you to learn. Worth a quick chat? Happy to show you exactly what it does first.
```

## The warm opener (to your L2 buyers)
```
Hey [NAME] — saw you grabbed the Lead Response Kit. Setting it up yourself, or would you
rather I just install the whole thing for you this week? Flat $490, done in 72 hours, and
you never touch the tech. Reply "done for me" and I'll send the details.
```

---

## The 10-minute discovery call (structure)

1. **Ask about the pain (let them talk):**
   - "How are leads reaching you right now — calls, texts, Facebook?"
   - "When you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that lead?"
   - "How do you currently ask for reviews?"
2. **Reflect it back:** "So a chunk of people who call while you're working just… ring the next
   business. And reviews only happen when you remember. That's exactly what this fixes."
3. **Show, don't pitch:** share your screen or a 60-second video of the auto-text firing.
4. **State the offer plainly:** "$490, I build the whole thing in 72 hours, you get a 20-minute
   handover and a cheat sheet. 7-day guarantee — if it's not catching leads, you don't pay."
5. **Ask for the sale:** "Want me to get started this week?"
6. **Onboard on the spot:** send the onboarding form + invoice/deposit link right then.

---

## Objection handling

**"It's too expensive."**
> "Totally fair. What's one job worth to you — $300? $800? This pays for itself the first time
> it catches a lead you'd have missed. And it's a one-off, not a monthly agency bill."

**"I can probably set it up myself."**
> "You absolutely can — that's what the $59 kit is for. Most owners just don't have a spare
> afternoon, so I do it for you and you skip straight to the results. Your call."

**"How do I know it'll work?"**
> "Two things: I'll show you it running before you pay a cent, and there's a 7-day money-back
> guarantee. If it's not catching leads, email me and it's free."

**"I don't have time to deal with tech."**
> "That's exactly why this exists — you don't touch anything. I set it up, you get a 20-minute
> walkthrough, done."

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Of course. Can I send you a 60-second video of it in action so you can picture it? And I've
> got two setup slots this week — want me to hold one, no obligation?"

---

## Pricing tactics that work
- **Take a deposit to book** (50% or the full $490). It commits them and protects your time.
- **Offer only 2 setup slots per week** — real scarcity, and it keeps your delivery quality high.
- **Always mention the guarantee** — it removes the risk that stalls the sale.
- **At handover, pitch the $290/month Care plan** while they're happy and it's fresh.
