# L3 — The 72-Hour Delivery Process

Your repeatable delivery checklist. Same steps every client = fast, consistent, ~3.5 hours work.
Copy this checklist per client (duplicate it, tick as you go).

---

## Before the clock starts
- [ ] Deposit / payment received
- [ ] Onboarding form completed
- [ ] Required access confirmed (Section 5 of the form)

> The 72 hours starts when you have **payment + form + access** — not before. State this up front.

---

## Hour 0–1 — Kickoff & personalise (30–60 min)
- [ ] Read the onboarding form; note their trade, tone, rates, areas
- [ ] Duplicate your master `Leads` Google Sheet → rename for the client
- [ ] Personalise all 30 prompts (Module 1): drop in business name, trade, suburb, rates, voice
  - *Shortcut: paste their details + your master prompts into your AI chatbot and ask it to fill every bracket at once*
- [ ] Save the personalised prompt pack as their handover cheat sheet (1–2 pages)

## Hour 1–3 — Build the automations (~2 hrs)
- [ ] **Missed-call-to-text:** set up on their phone (Module 2 Level A) and/or SMS tool (Level B)
- [ ] **Leads sheet capture:** connect their main lead source → Make.com → sheet (Module 2 Level B)
- [ ] **Review engine:** build Sequence 1 with their Google review link (Module 3)
- [ ] **Quote follow-up:** build the 3-touch Sequence 2 (Module 3)
- [ ] Set friendly, on-brand copy in every automated message (use their personalised prompts)

## Hour 3–4 — Test everything (30 min)
- [ ] Send a dummy enquiry → confirm auto-text fires and row lands in the sheet
- [ ] Mark a test job `Completed` → confirm review request sends
- [ ] Set a test `Quote sent` date in the past → confirm follow-up triggers
- [ ] Screen-record the working system as proof + their future reference
- [ ] Fix anything that didn't fire; re-test

## Handover (20-min call)
- [ ] Walk them through the Leads sheet and each automation
- [ ] Show the 1-page cheat sheet; explain the Monday 15-min routine (Module 4)
- [ ] Send: cheat sheet + test recording + "what to do if X" quick FAQ
- [ ] **Pitch Tradiepilot Care ($290/month)** — monitoring, review replies, monthly re-engagement
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial once they've seen it work (schedule a check-in in ~2 weeks)

---

## Quality bar (don't skip)
- Every automation **tested with a real dummy lead** before handover — never hand over untested.
- Every automated message reads like the owner wrote it, not a robot.
- Client can explain the Monday routine back to you in one sentence. If not, re-explain.

## After delivery
- [ ] Day 2: quick "all working?" text
- [ ] Day 14: check-in → ask for testimonial → offer Care plan again if not taken
- [ ] Remove your access if they didn't take Care (per your privacy promise)
- [ ] Log the client + outcome in your own tracker (for referrals and case studies)

---

## Time & margin per client
| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Kickoff + personalise | 0.75 hr |
| Build automations | 2.0 hr |
| Test | 0.5 hr |
| Handover | 0.35 hr |
| **Total** | **~3.5 hr for $490** (~$140/hr) |

The more setups you do, the faster you get — many drop to ~2.5 hours once your templates are dialled in.
