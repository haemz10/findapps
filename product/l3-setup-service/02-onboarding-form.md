# L3 — Client Onboarding Form

Send this the moment they say yes. Build it as a **Google Form** or **Tally.so** (both free)
so answers land in a sheet automatically. Everything you need to deliver is here — collect it
once, so you never chase the client mid-build.

> Intro line for the form:
> *"Thanks for coming on board! Fill this in and I'll have your lead-response system live within
> 72 hours. Takes about 5 minutes — the more detail, the better your setup."*

---

## Section 1 — Business basics
1. Business name
2. Your name & best contact number
3. Trade / services offered
4. Suburbs / areas you cover
5. Business email
6. Website (if any)

## Section 2 — How leads reach you *(this shapes the build)*
7. How do customers usually contact you? *(tick all: phone call / SMS / website form / Facebook / Instagram / email / other)*
8. Which is your **main** source of new enquiries?
9. Roughly how many enquiries a week?
10. What number should auto-replies come from? *(your mobile / a business line)*

## Section 3 — Your voice & pricing *(so prompts sound like you)*
11. How would you describe how you talk to customers? *(casual / professional / blunt / warm — give an example if you can)*
12. Your standard call-out fee (AUD)
13. Your hourly rate or typical job pricing (AUD)
14. Any lines you always say / never say?
15. Emergency contact info for after-hours auto-replies

## Section 4 — Reviews & Google
16. Google Business Profile link (or "help me find/set it up")
17. Roughly how many Google reviews now?
18. Do you have a review link already? *(paste it, or "not sure")*

## Section 5 — Access needed *(for the automations)*
19. Can you share a Google account (or create one) for the Leads sheet & automations? *(yes / need help)*
20. Which lead sources should I connect? *(from Q7 — max 2 in the standard package)*
21. Preferred handover call time *(2–3 options)*

## Section 6 — Anything else
22. Anything specific you're hoping this fixes?
23. Anything I should know before I start?

---

### Access checklist (you fill this in, not the client)
Collect securely — never store passwords in the sheet. Use a shared Google account or
temporary access, and confirm you'll remove your access after the Care plan ends (if not ongoing).

- [ ] Google account access (Sheets + Forms)
- [ ] Google Business Profile access or review link
- [ ] Enquiry source access (website form / Facebook Page / email forwarding)
- [ ] SMS tool account (if using paid auto-text) — set up under *their* billing, not yours
- [ ] Deposit / payment received before build starts

> Privacy note to give the client: *"I'll only use the access needed to build your system, I
> won't store your passwords, and I'll remove my access when we're done unless you're on the
> monthly Care plan."*
