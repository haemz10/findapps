# L3 — Done-For-You Setup Service ($490)
## Service Overview & Positioning

The $59 kit sells itself to DIY-minded owners. But many local business owners **don't want
to build it themselves** — they want it done. That's who this is for, and it's where the
real money is: a handful of $490 clients closes most of your $3,000/month.

> Revenue math: **6 setups/month = $2,940**. Or **3 setups + 4 monthly-care clients** (below).
> You already own the delivery assets — this is the $59 kit, installed for them.

---

## The offer, in one sentence

> "We'll install a lead-response system in your business in 72 hours — so every enquiry gets
> answered in 60 seconds, quotes go out faster, and reviews come in automatically. Flat $490,
> done-for-you, nothing to learn."

---

## What's included (fixed scope — protect this)

| Deliverable | What you actually do |
|---|---|
| Missed-call-to-text | Set up their phone + (if paid tier) an SMS auto-responder |
| Lead capture sheet | Build their `Leads` Google Sheet, connected to their enquiry sources |
| Review engine | Wire up the review-request sequence with their Google link |
| Quote follow-up | Build the 3-touch quote-rescue automation |
| Prompt pack, personalised | Fill the 30 prompts with their business, trade, suburb, rates |
| 20-min handover call | Walk them through it; hand over a 1-page cheat sheet |

**Not included** (name these explicitly to prevent scope creep): website builds, ongoing
management, ad campaigns, CRM migration, more than 2 lead sources. Those are add-ons or the
monthly plan.

---

## Why you can deliver it in 72 hours

Because **80% is the kit you already built.** Per client you're doing:
- 30 min: onboarding form review + kickoff
- ~2 hrs: personalise prompts, build their sheet + 3 automations (reuse your templates)
- 30 min: test everything with dummy leads
- 20 min: handover call

**~3.5 hours of work for $490** = ~$140/hr effective. That's the whole point of a
productized service: same deliverable every time, priced by value not hours.

---

## The value ladder continues here

- **Order bump:** offer "+$200 and we'll also connect your website form & Facebook leads."
- **The recurring upsell (this is the gold):** at handover, offer **ReplyFast Care — $290/month**:
  we monitor the automations, reply to reviews, send monthly re-engagement blasts, and tweak as
  they grow. *This turns one-off setups into MRR* — the fastest path to a stable $3,000/month.

**Blended target for $3,000/month:**
- 3 setups ($490) = $1,470 + 5 Care clients ($290) = $1,450 → **$2,920**, and Care compounds
  every month while setups refill it.

---

## Positioning & price confidence

- **Don't compete with agencies on features — compete on speed and simplicity.** Agencies charge
  $2,000+ and take weeks. You're $490 and 72 hours.
- **Never hourly-quote this.** Flat fee. If a client is unusually complex, that's a "let's talk"
  custom quote, not a discount on this.
- **Anchor against the cost of the problem:** "One missed job is worth more than this setup."
