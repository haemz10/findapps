## After the sequence
- **Non-buyers** → move to a monthly "value newsletter" (one tip/prompt a month, occasional offer).
- **Buyers** → tag them, exit this sequence, send the Full Kit onboarding + a 2-week check-in that
  pitches the $490 Done-For-You setup or Care plan.

### Benchmarks to expect (don't panic if lower early)
- Free → paid conversion: **2–5%** is healthy for a cold list; warmer traffic converts higher.
- If opens are low → fix subject lines. If opens are fine but no clicks → fix the offer/CTA.
